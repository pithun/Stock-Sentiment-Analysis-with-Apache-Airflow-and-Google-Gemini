### 4. Which Would You Advise Me to Keep an Eye On

These stocks present compelling narratives with significant upside potential but also identifiable risks or complexities that warrant close observation.

*   **Broadcom (NASDAQ:AVGO):** Despite posting its ninth consecutive EPS beat, guiding AI revenue up 236% YOY, and receiving bullish analyst upgrades (e.g., Rosenblatt's $600 target implies 77% upside), the stock continues to slide (down 13.49% over the past month, 1.54% YTD). This extreme disconnect between strong fundamentals and price action makes it a potential contrarian buy if the market eventually re-rates it to align with its AI leadership and consistent performance.
*   **Oracle (NYSE:ORCL):** Similar to Broadcom, Oracle's stock is falling significantly (down 51.46% YOY, 8.95% weekly) despite reporting a "loudest cloud quarter" with revenue up 29.6%, cloud infrastructure growing 121%, and a staggering $664 billion AI backlog. The market is focused on negative free cash flow and high capex, but bulls see a massive backlog conversion opportunity. If Oracle executes on its backlog and returns to positive free cash flow, the current valuation could be a "generational buying opportunity."
*   **SoFi Technologies (NASDAQ:SOFI):** The stock has had a "punishing 2026," down 34.8% YTD and near 52-week lows. However, the business posted a record quarter, raised full-year guidance, and management sees it as an "inflection point." With rising 2027 EPS estimates and strong growth forecasts, it offers a compelling risk-reward if the market begins to re-rate it based on its operational execution rather than current sentiment.
*   **Generac Holdings (NYSE:GNRC):** While up 16% today on a major multi-year Amazon contract for data center generators, the deal includes warrants for Amazon, raising concerns about potential dilution. Also, peer stocks like Cummins remained flat, suggesting the rally is company-specific rather than a sector-wide re-rating. Watch how the warrant math plays out and if Generac can secure additional hyperscaler contracts to validate the long-term growth story beyond the initial Amazon deal.
*   **Nokia (NYSE:NOK):** The stock has rallied significantly (up 132.81% YOY, 65.14% YTD) and received a "strong buy" call from Jim Cramer, boosted by a Microsoft partnership for AI-powered telecom networks. However, Q2 saw an operating income loss and negative free cash flow due to restructuring charges. The balance between promising AI growth and ongoing restructuring challenges, plus the "Inverse Cramer" sentiment, makes it a fascinating watch for its turnaround potential.
*   **Snowflake (NYSE:SNOW):** The company is executing exceptionally well with accelerating product revenue growth and strong AI adoption, but its stock has already rallied over 50% YTD and trades at very high valuations (e.g., 167x forward earnings) with deeply negative GAAP economics. It's currently a "Hold" recommendation. Watch for sustained GAAP profitability or a significant pullback to improve the risk/reward for new positions.
