### 4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On

*   **Datadog:** While it experienced a significant drop due to a major customer reducing usage, its consumption-based model could still benefit from broader AI adoption. The upcoming print will be crucial to see if this was an isolated incident or a broader trend.
*   **HubSpot:** Plunged due to disappointing customer additions and a lowered forecast. If the reasons cited (larger buying committees, budget scrutiny) are cyclical and stabilize, the stock could present a rebound opportunity. Monitor net customer additions and revenue retention closely.
*   **Figma:** Despite strong underlying growth, the stock is down significantly due to a conservative Q3 guide and margin pressure from AI costs. If it can address the AI cost monetization challenge or if the market has overreacted, it could be an interesting play.
*   **Workday:** The acquisition talks with Silver Lake make it a key stock to watch. Any confirmed deal could significantly impact its valuation.
*   **Adobe:** News of "major changes after years of stock pain" suggests a strategic pivot. It's crucial to monitor what these changes entail and how they are received by the market, as they could signal a turnaround or further challenges.
*   **McDonald’s Corporation (MCD):** Despite recent stock weakness and a social media incident, Wall Street analysts maintain a significantly higher average 12-month target price. This divergence could indicate a potential value opportunity if the stock stabilizes and fundamentals remain strong.
