### **4. Which Stocks to Keep an Eye On:**

*   **Intel (INTC):** Despite a strong comeback (up 326% over the last year), skepticism remains due to foundry losses and high valuation. Watch if Data Center and AI revenue growth (59% YoY) and ASIC business expansion can sustain, and if foundry losses narrow. The $200 price target by 2028 is ambitious but possible if key milestones are met.
*   **Bitmine Immersion Technologies (BMNR):** This crypto treasury company is accumulating Ethereum heavily, pushing its stock up. However, it's a high-beta play directly tied to the volatile performance of Ethereum, and funding strategies (potential dilution) are key. Monitor ETH's price action and BMNR's next funding methods.
*   **Meta Platforms (META):** Facing a conundrum of collapsing free cash flow and heavy AI capital expenditures, yet analysts are positive about its new Muse AI agent. The success of consumer-facing AI products will determine if its massive infrastructure spending translates into revenue.
*   **Alphabet (GOOGL/GOOG):** Similar to Meta, it's investing heavily in AI infrastructure, impacting its short-term performance. However, strategists consider it "most interesting" due to its AI infrastructure ownership and diverse monetization paths, trading at a cheaper P/E than peers. Watch for clearer monetization strategies.
*   **AST SpaceMobile (ASTS) & Rocket Lab (RKLB):** These space stocks were dragged down by SpaceX's insider unlock today but have shown strong underlying momentum (buy calls, record backlogs, acquisitions). Keep an eye on their recovery once the SpaceX selling pressure subsides.
*   **PepsiCo (PEP):** Jim Cramer argues it's a smart buy despite recent underperformance compared to Coca-Cola, citing its lower valuation and 4% dividend yield. Monitor if its strategy for volume growth and cost management pays off to justify the "value" thesis.
*   **Palantir Technologies (PLTR):** Presented conflicting signals today; ARK Invest is shedding shares, but the company announced a new partnership with Nebius Group for sovereign AI infrastructure. Watch for further developments on this partnership and institutional interest.
*   **Oracle (ORCL) & Adobe (ADBE):** Both have earnings reports coming up. Oracle has a massive backlog, but its stock is underperforming. These reports will be critical in clarifying their future trajectories.
*   **Ethereum (ETH):** Bitmine's heavy accumulation highlights corporate interest, but the wider market's sustained demand, especially through institutional channels and ETFs, remains crucial for its long-term price recovery from past declines.
