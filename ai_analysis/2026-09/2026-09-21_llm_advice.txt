### **4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On**

*   **Super Micro Computer (SMCI):** A high-risk, high-reward AI server play. The company reported a blowout quarter and strong fiscal 2027 revenue guidance, yet the stock trades below analyst targets and insiders are selling. Concerns exist about margin durability, negative cash flow, an unresolved board review, and customer concentration. *Watch Q1 fiscal 2027 results and any updates on the board review for clarity.*
*   **Eli Lilly (LLY):** While fundamentally strong, its stock has been digesting premium valuations. Key factors to watch are the progress of its retatrutide BLA pathway, expansion of Foundayo's prescriber base, and any significant competitive moves from Novo Nordisk. *A 30% gain to $1,500 by 2027 is considered realistic if these catalysts align.*
*   **JPMorgan Chase (JPM) & Regional Banking Sector (KRE):** The initial negative reaction to the Fed hike suggests the "easy money" is gone. Keep an eye on Q3 results to see if net interest margins expand and card losses stabilize, or if deposit repricing and charge-offs accelerate, potentially signaling deeper problems.
*   **Critical Metals (CRML):** Its 25% surge is driven by a geopolitical headline, but the company is pre-revenue, and the security pact doesn't directly grant new mining rights or funding. *Monitor the official signing and text of the U.S.-Greenland agreement and the company's definitive feasibility study targeted by the end of 2026. This is highly speculative.*
*   **Meta Platforms (META):** The strong rally is largely based on the perceived success of its Muse AI agent and an analyst upgrade that came *before* the Connect developer conference. *Watch for concrete usage metrics and model details from the Connect conference this week, as these will either validate or undermine the current bullish sentiment.*
*   **Amazon (AMZN):** The company is making massive capital investments in AI infrastructure, which is highly bullish long-term but involves significant near-term cash burn and free cash flow headwinds. *Closely monitor AWS monetization, custom silicon scaling, and management's commentary on free cash flow in upcoming reports.*
*   **Rocket Lab (RKLB) & Intuitive Machines (LUNR):** The space sector is hot, but RKLB's recent catalyst was a reiteration, not an upgrade, and key events (Neutron debut, Iridium acquisition) lack firm timelines. LUNR's stronger surge without news suggests broader sentiment. *Monitor for concrete timelines for RKLB's catalysts and observe if the space cluster's gains hold after Friday's giveback, indicating accumulation vs. trading.*
*   **Visa (VISA):** Despite strong revenue growth and AI integration efforts, operating margins slipped, and free cash flow saw a sharp dip in one quarter. *Watch the next two to three quarters to see if operating expense growth decelerates and if free cash flow margins consistently recover above 50%, as this will determine if the reinvestment cycle is temporary or a more durable cost.*
