### 4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On

These stocks present a mix of strong potential and significant risks or complex dynamics that warrant close monitoring:

*   **Merck (MRK):** The stock has seen stellar performance (+84% in 12 months) driven by a promising pipeline (e.g., LIPFENDRA approval, positive Phase III results). However, its underlying profitability (10.5% operating margin) is among the lowest in its peer group, raising questions about market pricing relative to earnings power and immense execution risk for its post-KEYTRUDA vision. **Watch for:** Early adoption and sales trajectory of LIPFENDRA for concrete signs of pipeline conversion to profit.
*   **Palantir (PLTR):** Reported "otherworldly" Q2 revenue growth (92.83% YoY), especially in U.S. commercial AI (149%), and expanded alliances. Despite this, the stock sits 8% below its high, faces skepticism over its high valuation (P/E 255), insider selling, and renewed short calls from Michael Burry regarding receivables quality. **Watch for:** Continued U.S. commercial TCV growth, resolution of receivables quality concerns, and progress on sovereign AI roadmap.
*   **Meta (META):** Stock is down sharply, burdened by ballooning AI capex ($130-145 billion) and legal charges, which have squeezed free cash flow. Yet, its underlying "AI flywheel" is compounding, driving strong ad revenue growth (27.96% YoY), and analysts see a path to $1,000. **Watch for:** Q3 revenue guidance, flattening CapEx growth into 2027, and the outcomes of youth-safety litigation.
*   **Dell Technologies (DELL):** Posted a massive 15.81% surge on record AI-optimized server revenue and raised FY27 revenue outlook. However, gross margins are under pressure (21.1%), and free cash flow fell significantly, primarily due to rising memory costs. The stock has run up nearly 300% YTD. **Watch for:** Stabilization of memory costs and whether Dell can convert its record backlog into improved profitability rather than just top-line growth.
*   **Amazon.com (AMZN):** Down 10% over the past month and lagging the S&P 500, with increasing capital spending projections ($220 billion in 2026). However, its AWS cloud unit is accelerating (36.7% YoY revenue growth, $496 billion backlog), and overall operating margin is at a three-year peak. **Watch for:** How efficiently Amazon's massive AI infrastructure spending translates into monetizable services and free cash flow recovery.
*   **Vertiv (VRT):** Despite consistently beating earnings, climbing estimates, and very bullish analyst targets (one at $500, implying 95% upside), the stock has shed nearly a third of its value from its peak due to "timing shifts" in Q2 revenue and profit-taking. It's a key player in AI data center power and cooling. **Watch for:** Q3 organic growth (guided 34-36%), resolution of timing shifts, and liquid-cooling attach rates for NVIDIA platforms.
