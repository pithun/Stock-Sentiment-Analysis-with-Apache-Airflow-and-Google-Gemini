### 4. Which Stocks Would You Advise Me to Keep an Eye On?

Based on today's news and underlying trends, here are a few stocks to watch closely:

*   **Dell Technologies (DELL):** With a massive AI backlog and demand outstripping supply, Dell is a strong play in the AI infrastructure build-out. Its significant returns make it compelling, but monitor for potential supply chain constraints easing.
*   **Micron (MU):** Its server memory breakthrough and validation from major players like AMD and Intel position it well for future growth in the AI space. The stock has seen incredible momentum, but future revenue depends on successful qualification and production.
*   **Arista Networks (ANET):** As a "direct bet on AI build-out" with strong ties to hyperscalers like Microsoft and Meta, Arista Networks is an interesting high-growth, high-volatility play. Watch its ability to maintain high revenue guidance amidst supply constraints.
*   **Lumentum Holdings (LITE):** As a leader in the surging optics sector, fueled by AI-driven demand, Lumentum's impressive year-to-date gains make it a crucial stock to track. However, be mindful of its stretched valuation if optical capital expenditure intentions cool.
*   **Revolut:** Its plans for a dual public listing are significant. While not yet traded, its high valuation and aggressive European expansion make it a potential high-profile IPO/listing to watch once details emerge.
*   **Walt Disney (DIS):** This stock presents a mixed picture but could be a compelling watch for value investors. Despite recent underperformance, Disney is investing heavily in its Experiences division and guiding for double-digit EPS growth. If these investments pay off, the stock could see a turnaround from its current discount.
*   **T-Mobile US (TMUS):** Although currently facing bad sentiment, T-Mobile's situation with potential M&A (Deutsche Telekom) and its internal strategy changes could lead to significant shifts. Keep an eye on any developments that might signal a stabilization or reversal of its current downward trend.
