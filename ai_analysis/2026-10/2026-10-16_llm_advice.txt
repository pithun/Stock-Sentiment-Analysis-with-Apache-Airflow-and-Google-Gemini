### **4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On**

1.  **CoreWeave (and related AI infrastructure debt):** This company is a significant indicator of the health of AI infrastructure financing. Its tumbling shares, surging bond yields, and the CEO's stock sales, coupled with Nvidia's stake and the possibility of a buyout, make it a high-stakes play. The "contagion effect" on Nebius Group, Applied Digital, and IREN means its performance will ripple through the sector. Pay close attention to how its debt situation evolves and any further statements from Nvidia.
2.  **Strategy (MSTR) & Metaplanet (3350):** The MSCI index exclusion risk is monumental. If these companies are removed, it will severely impact demand from index-tracking funds. Watch for their strategies to mitigate this, any new investor bases, and whether their Bitcoin purchasing strategy can sustain itself without easy share issuance. This could be a significant turning point for "Bitcoin treasury" companies.
3.  **Anthropic & Broader Chipmakers (e.g., Lam Research, Texas Instruments, Dell Technologies):** Anthropic's financial growth slowdown concerns are directly impacting chipmakers, the backbone of the AI boom. Upcoming earnings from chip companies like Lam Research and Texas Instruments will be crucial for gauging real demand and investor confidence in the AI buildout beyond the initial euphoria. The market is "jittery," and these reports will provide clarity.
4.  **Apple:** While projected for market share growth, the "lukewarm reception" for iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max and "hinge constraints" for iPhone Duo suggest potential headwinds. Its ability to navigate production challenges and maintain strong demand will be key to sustaining its growth trajectory as the global smartphone market shrinks.
5.  **Tesla (TSLA):** The flying Roadster news is exciting, but upcoming Q3 earnings (Oct 21) are the next major catalyst. Tesla is always volatile, and its earnings reports often set the tone for the broader EV and tech sectors.
6.  **Meta Platforms:** As one of the "hyperscalers" with massive capital spending on AI data centers, Meta's upcoming earnings will be vital. Investors are eager to see if the profitability can grow faster than its capital expenditure, which will determine confidence in the sustainability of the AI investment boom.
7.  **L&T Finance (LTF) / Angel One / Mankind Pharma (Indian Market):** These companies appeared on a "top losers" list but also reported solid Q2 earnings (L&T, Angel One). This discrepancy suggests that they might be oversold, potentially presenting an opportunity for investors who look beyond immediate price drops to underlying financial performance. Deeper analysis into why they dipped despite good earnings is warranted.
