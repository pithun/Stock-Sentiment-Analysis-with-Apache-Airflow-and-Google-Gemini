**4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On:**

*   **Reddit (RDDT):** While S&P 500 inclusion is a massive boost, monitor the ongoing negotiations for its data licensing deal with Google (Reddit wants usage-based pricing) and the impact of Google's AI Overviews on its search referrals. These will be critical for sustained long-term growth.
*   **Workday:** The potential private buyout by Silver Lake is a major development. Watch for any official announcements or competing bids. However, also keep an eye on how the underlying market concerns about AI's impact on subscription software models evolve, as this could dictate its performance if the deal doesn't materialize.
*   **SpaceX:** The multiple upcoming lockup expirations throughout the year could introduce significant selling pressure. Monitor how the stock reacts to these events, as they will test investor demand and the stock's stability.
*   **Dell Technologies (DELL):** With recent analyst upgrades and a technical breakout, Dell could be poised for further gains, especially if the AI infrastructure buildout continues strong.
*   **Nvidia (NVDA):** As a leader in AI, its continued expansion into new areas like robotics will be key. Any news regarding new partnerships or product developments could further drive its valuation.
*   **AI Sector (general):** Given Michael Burry's dire warnings, it's prudent to watch the broader AI market for signs of unsustainable valuations, oversupply in compute capacity, and any shifts in capital expenditure trends. This isn't about specific stocks, but the health of the entire ecosystem.
