#### 4. Which Would You Advise Me to Keep an Eye On:

*   **SpaceX (SPCX):** This stock is a prime example of high risk, high reward. While it saw a significant plunge today due to massive AI investments and a net loss, it also beat revenue expectations, and its Starlink connectivity business is booming. Many Wall Street analysts remain highly bullish, seeing over 100% upside. The volatility from AI capex concerns and the upcoming lock-up expiry creates a "buy-the-dip" opportunity for investors with a long-term, high-conviction view on its ambitious space and AI ventures.
*   **AMD (AMD):** Despite falling today due to market expectations and capex, AMD has a strong long-term AI strategy, leveraging its open-source ROCm software against Nvidia's CUDA moat. The company beat revenue expectations and has seen substantial growth over the past year. Today's dip could be a tactical entry point for those believing in AMD's ability to capture a larger share of the AI chip market.
*   **Circle Internet Group (CRCL):** Although the stock is down today and has had a tough year (down 60% over 12 months), its strategic moves are significant. Securing the first federal bank charter for a stablecoin company and enlisting major players like BlackRock, Visa, and Mastercard as validators for its Arc network are strong long-term signals. If these strategic developments translate into market share gains and operational efficiencies, the current dip could offer a compelling entry for investors interested in the digital asset infrastructure space.
*   **Match Group (MTCH):** While overall sentiment is negative due to declining paying users and cautious guidance, there are green shoots. Tinder showed its strongest operating trend in ten quarters, and Hinge is expanding rapidly. If management can effectively address the broader user base decline and the Azar app disruption, these segment strengths could signal a potential turnaround. Keep an eye on future updates regarding user engagement and retention strategies.
