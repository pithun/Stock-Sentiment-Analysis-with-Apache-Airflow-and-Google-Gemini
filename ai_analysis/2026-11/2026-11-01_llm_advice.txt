*   **Skydance:** Despite filing a shelf registration for employee stock ownership, its share price has been down 16.32% over the last 30 days, indicating recent negative market performance.

**3. Stocks with a Good Sentiment:**

**4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On:**

*   **Netflix (NFLX):** The stock hit a new 52-week low and is down 33% year-to-date. However, the news highlights that it's "still growing revenue by double digits" and is "a stock trading at a discount to the market," suggesting a potential buying opportunity for value investors if its growth trajectory remains strong.
*   **Aave / Coinbase:** Aave's new "Equities Hub" allows borrowing against tokenized U.S. equities from Coinbase. This represents an interesting intersection of traditional finance and DeFi. While the long-term economics and risks are still being assessed, this innovation could signal a significant trend in digital assets and collateralized lending.
*   **Amazon, Meta, Oracle (and the broader AI Infrastructure sector):** These tech giants, along with Alphabet and Microsoft, are committing nearly $700 billion combined to AI capital expenditures this year. However, market commentary questions whether the returns will justify such massive sums. While they are driving innovation, the high spending and associated $1.65 trillion in off-balance-sheet debt tied to AI infrastructure make their future performance highly dependent on AI monetization. Watch for clearer signals on their return on AI investment.
*   **General AI and Chip Stocks:** The sector has experienced a rough October, with warnings from Google's CEO about a potential market correction reminiscent of the dot-com bubble. Continued volatility and shifting market sentiment in this high-growth sector warrant close monitoring.
