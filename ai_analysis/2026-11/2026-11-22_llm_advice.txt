### 2. Stocks with Bad Sentiment:

*   **Nvidia:** Experienced a sharp decline ($1.7 trillion market cap loss, 30% drop), worst weekly performance since April 2025. This was exacerbated by CEO Jensen Huang's resignation due to personal misconduct and a reported regulatory investigation into its accounting practices. The sentiment is broadly bearish despite strong projected revenue growth, as leadership uncertainty and broader AI market skepticism weigh heavily.
*   **Nasdaq Composite & Nasdaq-100:** Down 20% and approaching bear market territory, with the Nasdaq Composite dropping 5.3% on Friday and 10% for the week, its worst weekly performance since March 2020. It has lost all its 2026 gains.
*   **Dow Jones Industrial Average:** Entered correction territory (down 10%) with an 8% loss, its worst weekly drop since 2020.
*   **S&P 500:** Also entered correction territory (down 10%), experienced a 3.7% drop on Friday, extending its losing streak to seven sessions.
*   **Coinbase:** Hovering near its 52-week low, down approximately 64% from its 52-week high. It faces significant transaction revenue declines (down 44% YoY) and overall revenue drops. Analyst downgrades (Morgan Stanley to Underweight) and a competitor's security breach are further dampening confidence.
*   **Workday:** Has slumped into a bear market, falling nearly 25% from its year-to-date high.
*   **KOSPI (South Korea) & Taiex (Taiwan):** Both Asian chipmaking indices experienced significant drops (Kospi -4%, Taiex -6%), prompting South Korea's finance ministry to prepare market stabilization funds. This reflects a broader negative sentiment impacting semiconductor-heavy markets.
*   **AI Sector (General):** Faced widespread bearishness due to concerns about high capital expenditure for data centers, potential cash shortages for AI companies (as voiced by Elon Musk), unproven long-term profitability, and fears of an "AI bubble" bursting.

---

### 3. Stocks with Good Sentiment:

*   **None explicitly positive for individual stocks today.** Most news focuses on the broader market downturn and AI sector challenges.
*   **General Market Outlook (2027):** Despite the current correction, several financial institutions (Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, UBS, Wells Fargo, Federated Hermes, Modera Wealth Management) are projecting solid gains for the S&P 500 in 2027, dismissing the current selloff as an "emotional" or "garden variety correction" rather than a fundamental bust.

### 4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On:

*   **Anthropic:** Despite a "lacklustre post-IPO performance" that contributed to AI trade pressure, its IPO was record-breaking, raising significant capital ($81 billion, opened well above offering price). However, it carries massive unfunded spending commitments ($518 billion) and reported a substantial net loss. Its ability to navigate intense competition and demonstrate sustainable profitability will be crucial.
*   **Nvidia:** While currently facing severe headwinds from its CEO scandal, regulatory scrutiny, and a significant stock drop, it still holds a dominant 94% share of the GPU market. Analysts are divided, with some lowering price targets but maintaining "buy" ratings. Its recovery hinges on new leadership stabilizing the company and continued demand for its core products amidst broader AI market skepticism.
*   **The broader AI Sector (including Meta, Microsoft, Google, OpenAI):** The current market correction is significantly driven by concerns over AI investments' costs and future profitability. The performance of key AI players and the sustainability of data center expansion will dictate whether this is a temporary shakeout or the beginning of a larger downturn for the sector.
*   **S&P 500 / Nasdaq / Dow:** These major indices are in correction territory, but analyst predictions for 2027 are surprisingly optimistic. Investors should monitor whether these indices find a bottom and if economic factors (inflation, interest rates, consumer spending) align with the projected rebound. The "AI bubble" debate will continue to be a significant factor.
