#### 4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On:

*   **Anthropic:** The highly anticipated IPO faces a mixed sentiment. While initial demand is robust, concerns about its long-term profitability and the broader "AI bubble" persist. Its performance in the coming days/weeks will be a critical indicator for the entire AI sector. Keep a close watch on how its stock and options trade post-IPO and if it can sustain its high valuation.
*   **Nvidia:** This stock presents a fascinating dichotomy: strong financial fundamentals (record revenue, optimistic forecasts) against a backdrop of significant leadership scandal and ongoing SEC/Congressional scrutiny. How the market balances these forces, and whether the interim leadership can effectively navigate the challenges while fending off rivals like AMD, will be crucial. Monitor for any further developments regarding its leadership and potential shifts in market dynamics for AI chips.
*   **CoreWeave:** As a "neocloud" company deeply integrated with Nvidia and facing broader industry concerns about high debt levels for AI capacity, CoreWeave's trajectory (and that of similar firms like Nebius and Iren) will offer insights into the sustainability and profitability of the AI infrastructure boom. Its ability to secure financing and prove ROI on AI investments will be a key indicator.
*   **Intel:** The massive stock drop and class-action lawsuit make Intel a high-risk, potentially high-reward play. For value investors, a 28.6% dip might signal an opportunity if they believe the company can overcome its PC slump and legal challenges. However, the depth of its issues demands thorough due diligence before considering any investment.
