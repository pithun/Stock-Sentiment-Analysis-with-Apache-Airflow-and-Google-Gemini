*   **Nvidia ($NVDA):** Shares fell nearly 5% due to concerns over its $750 billion investment plan, with critics highlighting potential "circular deals" where NVIDIA funds customers who then buy its chips, stretching its already high commitments.
*   **Shein:** Reported a quarterly loss, a significant decline from the previous year's income, with sales slowing due to the removal of a US import duty exemption and broader geopolitical uncertainties (US-China tariffs, Iran war impact).
*   **Qualcomm (QCOM):** The stock has fallen 33% from its 52-week high, trading at a steep discount. Operating margins have shrunk, 3-year growth is sluggish, and management lowered guidance, particularly in its core handset business in China.
*   **Dr Reddy's & Cipla:** Both Indian pharmaceutical firms experienced significant drops in net profits and operating margins for the June quarter due to declining US business, leading analysts to reduce earnings forecasts.
*   **MARA Holdings (MARA):** Mentioned as a less suitable acquisition candidate for CleanSpark due to lacking the necessary balance sheet and missing Q1 revenue expectations.
*   **Plug Power & Nel ASA:** Both hydrogen specialists are described as struggling, with Plug Power's share price in a noticeable technical downtrend, fighting for survival.

### **4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On:**

*   **CXMT:** While its IPO was spectacular, the initial surge was partially due to only 7% of shares being available. Monitor how the stock performs as more shares become tradable and if its valuation can sustain this initial frenzy. It signals strong demand for memory chips, but volatility is likely.
*   **Nvidia ($NVDA):** Despite today's dip and "circular deal" concerns, Nvidia remains a dominant force in AI chips. Keep a close watch on how the market reacts to future announcements regarding its investment strategies and supply commitments, as a temporary correction could present an opportunity if the underlying AI demand remains robust.
*   **Qualcomm (QCOM):** Currently undervalued due to handset weakness, but its diversification into automotive and AI is promising. Management expects the handset business to bottom in Q3. If they can execute on this stabilization and diversification, QCOM could be a strong turnaround play.
*   **CleanSpark (CLSK):** Trading at a "depressed price" despite owning significant and strategically scarce power/data center assets in high-demand regions (Texas, Georgia). Analysts see substantial upside (54%). It's a high-risk, high-reward stock to watch for potential acquisition by hyperscalers or a significant tenant lease.
*   **Palantir Technologies (PLTR):** The long-term growth story is compelling with surging demand for its AI Platform. Watch for continued strong U.S. commercial revenue growth as the key metric to validate its high valuation and unlock future returns.
*   **Shein:** Though currently facing headwinds and a quarterly loss, its impending IPO in Hong Kong will be a significant market event. Monitor the terms of the IPO and initial trading to gauge investor confidence in the fast-fashion giant's ability to navigate tariffs and competitive pressures.
