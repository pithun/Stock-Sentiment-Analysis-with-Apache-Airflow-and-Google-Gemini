### **4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On**

*   **Meta Platforms (META):** While its stock has pulled back, the underlying business appears strong with significant revenue growth and cash flow. Analysts view the dip as a potential buying opportunity, especially if upcoming earnings confirm that AI investments are boosting ad performance without eroding margins. It's a high-growth company at a potentially attractive valuation.
*   **Alphabet (GOOGL/GOOG):** Similar to Meta, Alphabet is investing heavily in AI and has faced stock pressure due to capital expenditure concerns. Its strong cloud growth is a positive, but investors are seeking clearer evidence of ROI from AI spending. Watch for signs that these investments are translating into stronger profits.
*   **Intel (INTC):** A stock experiencing significant volatility. Initially hit by AI spending concerns, it later saw its shares rise on strong AI-driven revenue and an improved outlook. The challenge for Intel remains the cost of competing in advanced chip production. Its ability to execute on its turnaround and manage its extensive capex will be crucial.
*   **Micron Technology (MU):** With Morgan Stanley highlighting unprecedented demand for data center memory and persistent shortages, Micron is in a unique cyclical upturn. While memory stocks can be volatile, this cycle appears fundamentally different due to AI. Keep an eye on supply-demand dynamics and pricing trends.
