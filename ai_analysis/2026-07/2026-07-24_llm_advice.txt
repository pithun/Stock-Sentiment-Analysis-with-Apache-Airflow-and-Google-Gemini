#### **4. Which stocks would you advise me to keep an eye on?**

Given the current market dynamics, particularly the AI bubble warnings and geopolitical tensions, here are some stocks and sectors to watch closely:

1.  **AI-Adjacent Tech (Meta, Microsoft, Amazon):** These "Magnificent 7" members are reporting earnings next week. The market is increasingly scrutinizing capital expenditure for AI. Any significant boost in spending could lead to investor disappointment and a stock sell-off, similar to what Alphabet and Tesla experienced. Their performance will be a key indicator of AI investment sentiment.
2.  **SK Hynix:** While its shares slumped due to a non-business-related divorce settlement for its chairman, the company has seen massive growth (nearly 150% YTD) driven by its role in supplying high-bandwidth memory (HBM) chips for AI supercomputers. If the divorce settlement impact is temporary and does not affect the company's fundamentals, the recent dip could present a strategic entry point for investors bullish on the long-term AI hardware trend, but only after careful due diligence.
3.  **Intuit (INTU):** The stock is facing extreme uncertainty, with a wide range of possible outcomes priced in by the options market. It's a battle between new, fast-growing segments (like TurboTax Live) and struggles in its legacy DIY tax business. Watching management's strategy to address the legacy issues will be crucial for its future direction. This could be a high-reward, high-risk situation.
4.  **eBay:** Despite lagging the S&P 500, eBay has generously returned cash to shareholders and is actively pursuing strategic priorities in collectibles, fashion, and C2C sales, deploying AI tools for seller engagement. The question is whether these new growth drivers can overcome intense competition and market perception as a "mature business." Watch for signs of sustained growth in its new initiatives to determine if it can escape its "market laggard" status.
5.  **SBI Cards & Payment Services:** Identified as showing signs of a turnaround after a significant drop. This warrants closer investigation into the catalysts for the potential recovery and whether the underlying business fundamentals support a sustained upward trend.
