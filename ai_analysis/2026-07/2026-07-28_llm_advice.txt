### 4. Stocks to Keep an Eye On

I'd advise keeping a close watch on these companies due to their significant developments, shifting market dynamics, or potential for future movements:

*   **Nvidia (NVDA):** While experiencing a sell-off due to AI infrastructure costs, Nvidia remains a powerhouse in the AI space. The current pullback might present a buying opportunity for long-term believers, especially if the market re-evaluates the return on AI investments. The competition and spending dynamics are key.
*   **Cracker Barrel (CBRL):** Despite the CEO departure due to past missteps, the company has reported better-than-expected revenue and raised its full-year outlook. The question is whether this recovery is sustainable and if the new leadership can drive consistent growth. A classic turnaround play.
*   **Lucid Motors (LCID):** A highly volatile stock that just saw a massive surge (20.5%) after a Saudi Prince disclosed a significant stake and bankruptcy rumors were dismissed. However, it's still deeply down year-over-year. This is a high-risk, high-reward stock – watch if the new investment can stabilize and fuel production.
*   **QuantumScape (QS):** This solid-state battery developer is deeply beaten down (50% YTD) but possesses genuinely scarce intellectual property and strong partnerships (Volkswagen, Honda, Corning). With ongoing cash burn, it's either a potential acquisition target for a major player or a high-risk, high-reward bet on a technological breakthrough. Watch for OEM production commitments or further investor interest.
*   **The "AI Premium" Beneficiaries (e.g., AppLovin (APP), Carvana (CVNA), Lumentum (LITE), Baker Hughes (BKR)):** News today highlights a shift in investor sentiment from pure AI *infrastructure* (like Nvidia) to companies expected to *benefit* from AI across various industries. These stocks are identified as having an "AI Premium" and could outperform as AI adoption matures beyond just chip sales. It's a key theme to observe.
